Here is the order draftwright itself uses, and the verdicts below rely on it. A line through a
label is a hard layout finding: `annotation_ink_overlap` and `leader_line_through_text` are in
`linting.quality._HARD_LAYOUT_CODES`, and the recovery will not take a sheet that has one. A leader
crossing a line is kept under Policy B and reported at info (`feature_leader_crossing`; Python's
ctc03 sheet has five). An annotation off the sheet is the worst of all, because it is not on the
drawing. The port follows the same order: `model::HARD_LAYOUT` holds `annotation_ink_overlap` and
`annotation_off_sheet` but not `annotation_leader_crossing`. The `annotation_outside_area` lines
are info: ink past the area reserved for a view's bands, which "may crowd what lies beyond".

Four changes were measured (`tools/quality.py` on the 30 parts each time):
1. **Kept: a leader through another leader's label weighs two crossings.** In the joint
   assignment (`dims::leaders::conflict`, `assign`, `Search`), two chosen leaders whose ink meets a

   two leaders whose lines only cross still count one. So the assignment lets two leaders cross

   counted one, and a pair running through each other's labels (two hard findings) cost the same as
   one crossing (info). Result: ctc02 ink overlaps 9 → 7 (its baseline), crossings 19 → 21 (baseline
